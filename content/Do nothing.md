"When you don't know what to do, do nothing."

I've also seen this as "when you don't know what to do, be still."

I haven't found a good source for the quote, but it seems like something Dallas Willard would say (if not in those exact words).

Willard gave the following advice for participants in a silent retreat: “Do nothing. Don’t try to make anything happen.”

Source: https://www.soulshepherding.org/productivity-starts-with-doing-nothing/
