“Remember that the words are of the first importance.”

- G.H. Palmer (1846-1926), Anglican

"Read singingly; sing readingly."

- Henry Walford Davies

“We speak in little groups of twos and threes, not a constant 3/4 or 4/4.”

- Alice Parker, composer

“Good chanting, whatever music is employed, must be as faithful to the language as good, clear reading.”

- Reginald Box, Anglican

The “amazing variability of speech has to be built into whatever language is sung. In our page-driven age, we tend to shove the word into the note value, as if the quarter note were an ice cube with the syllable frozen inside. I’d much rather think of the notation, both pitch and rhythm, as being at the service of the word or phrase as naturally spoken, with all the subtlety preserved. My inner vision is of a word, in its lumpy variability, being bathed in pitch and duration as though these were precious oils being poured over it.”

- Alice Parker, composer
