> “Sing and make melody in your hearts to the Lord” (Eph. 5:19). The new song is sung first in the heart. Otherwise it cannot be sung at all. The heart sings because it is overflowing with Christ. That is why all singing in the church is a spiritual performance. Surrender to the Word, incorporation in the community, great humility, and much discipline—there are the prerequisites of all singing together.


- Dietrich Bonhoeffer, Life Together, trans. John W. Doberstein, HarperOne, 1954, 58
