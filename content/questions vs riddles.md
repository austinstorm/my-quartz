“Questions are the engines of intellect, the cerebral machines which convert energy to motion, and curiosity to controlled inquiry.”

- David Fischer Hackett

“In genuine questioning, the questioner seeks some information of which he is ignorant, and which he believes is accessible to the addressee. In the case of riddles, however, the riddler is in possession of some information which he manipulates the addressee into seeking.”

- Shlomith Cohen, *Untying the Knot: On Riddles and Other Enigmatic Modes*

Or, in Katelijne Schiltz’s pithy description, “A riddle is a question which already contains the answer.”
