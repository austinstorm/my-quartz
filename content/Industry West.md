Industry West is the most cringe. Their whole business model is based on selling knock-offs of design classics, and they soon realized that the money was in hospitality projects because hospitality often only cares about 'the look' not provenance

I save this mainly to remind myself that if I build a business based on generic prouve and eames to not also be pretentious

> Believe or not, trend-forward design brand Industry West was founded a decade ago. Anne and Jordan England, the husband-and-wife founders, had just moved to South Carolina and were struggling to find furniture that fit their mid-century design style. That’s when Jordan looked overseas and found a factory in Asia that was crafting the modern-industrial furnishings he had in mind.
