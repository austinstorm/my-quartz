"this history [of the New Thought] might disappoint some readers, if they had made up their minds that it is necessary to look into the far past and discover ideas in India, in ancient Greece, in the Middle Ages, which resemble the therapeutic ideas of today."

- Horatio Wills Dresser

This is in the context of reckoning with The Kybalion, I think.

Each sect, or rather denominational flavor, of Christianity has its own particular appeal to authority. Greek root words, the early church, the reformation, &cet.
