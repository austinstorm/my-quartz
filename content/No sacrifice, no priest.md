> Where Anglican clergy disagrees with Rome, and is also arguably more faithful to the Scriptures, is when did the satisfaction for sins occur. For Romans, Christ’s sacrifice is repeatedly brought before the Father for the forgiveness of sins committed by the faithful and so its merits are applied today. In Anglicanism, it is different.
