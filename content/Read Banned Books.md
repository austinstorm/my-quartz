I've always been deeply skeptical of 'read banned books' as a slogan.

First, the most recent enthusiasts for 'banning' books have been the YA cancel squad. That is, the very sort of people who might have a 'read banned books' sticker were calling for the complete censorship of books because they were 'problematic'.

https://freddiedeboer.substack.com/p/after-liberal-cultural-hegemony

But even assuming that not all proponents of the slogan would have participated in the YA cancellation moment, one highly suspects they mean "Beloved" and not "Mein Kampf".

The ALA has always over-reported calls to censor books, falsely equating middle-school parents' desire that their children not see graphic novels with explicit sex with calls to 'ban' books.
