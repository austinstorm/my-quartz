> It’s ironic to realize now, especially considering the bad trips I had, that my psychedelic voyaging was actually a very escapist endeavour. It carried me farther away from authentic relationship, farther away from being in an incarnated body, farther away from the reality that God has ordained. And the reality that God has ordained is often saturated with tedium and suffering.


- Ashley Lande
