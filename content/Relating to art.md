> We have forgotten how to relate emotionally to art: we treat it like editors, searching in it for that which the artist has supposedly hidden.


- Andrey Tarkovski

This reminds me of C.S. Lewis:

> “The first demand any work of any art makes upon us is surrender. Look. Listen. Receive. Get yourself out of the way.”


And this anti-utilitarian sentiment:

> To value them [works of art] chiefly for reflections which they may suggest to us or morals we may draw from them, is a flagrant instance of “using” instead of receiving. … One of the prime achievements in every good fiction has nothing to do with truth or philosophy or a Weltanschauung at all.


- from his *An Experiment in Criticism*
