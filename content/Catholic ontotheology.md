I kinda thought this was a protestant thing? But interesting to note it appears in Catholicism. Vocation as a destiny

> Layered on top of this “ontotheology” is a particular way of talking about vocation that many of us absorbed long before seminary, which was then reinforced over years of spiritual direction and vocational testimonies once we arrived there. The basic story goes something like this: God has one specific state in life he is calling you to, and your task is to discover which one it is and give yourself to it without reserve. Once you have “found your vocation,” faithfulness means never turning back. The priesthood is not simply one possible path of discipleship among others; it is your path, the one God has chosen for you from all eternity.
