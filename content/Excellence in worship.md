“Congregational music does not have to be so immediate that no effort is needed to learn it and get it right. The principle of recitation, with an inflection on the final accent, is straightforward. People can grasp it quickly. But nothing is so instant that it requires no practice.”

“Worship means offering to God the best of which we are capable: what proclaims His worth. The best, and what is musically good, may well be simple. It will be what is within the capacity of the worshippers, be they choir or congregation, to do well and not badly. … Somebody remarked that God was not a music critic. What is utterly genuine may not always be musically impeccable. On the other hand worship does not mean giving to God what requires no care or trouble.”

- Reginald Box, Anglican
