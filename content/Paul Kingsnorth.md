> he has sought not only to diagnose the problem but also to live his own life as an exemplar of his proposed solution. For the Englishman Kingsnorth, this cultivation of a life lived locally means buying a farm in Ireland and joining the Romanian Orthodox Church, along with writing this book for an imprint of Penguin Random House and posting regular lamentations of the state of the West on his Substack. Kingsnorth’s is a book read, and a life lived, in a rather paradoxical fashion, but as Rosalie L. Colie noted in her 1966 classic, Paradoxica Epidemica: The Renaissance Tradition of Paradox, paradoxes exploit relative or competing value systems: ‘The paradox is always somehow involved in dialectic: challenging some orthodoxy, the paradox is an oblique criticism of absolute judgment or absolute convention’
