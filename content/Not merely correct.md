> The truth is that I do not want a Christianity that is merely correct anymore. Yes, I am still a committed Thomist, with an existentialist streak, but I think the is a means to a true end. Realism, properly understood, is not the disenchantment of things, but the recovery of their radiance. I want a radiant Christianity that is alive. I want a Christianity large enough for reverence and delight, doctrine and wonder, sacrament and song. I want the kind of romantic Christianity John Eldredge speaks of. That the heart is not an embarrassing defect to be managed, but part of what must be redeemed and offered back to God. This is not a sentimentality I am after, God, I hate that word. I wish for a faith in which the world is charged again, a luminous realism. A faith in which creation is not just matter but gift, given to me, given to you.


- Javier Valazquez
