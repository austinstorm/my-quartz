> If we can finally believe that God is always good, that life's true content is mercy and love, that the future is always worth waiting for, it is because there is one witness... who gave his back indeed to the smiter, who waited for God and only for God, who waited to the very end and who lives to bring his testimony. If we can finally believe that God is always good, it is because we rely on the testimony of one who bore God's affliction unto death, and knows that even that grief is the beginning of abundance.


- Robert Jenson
