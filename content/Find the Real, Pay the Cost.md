> There are only three ways to interact with any good: abstinence, moderation, and devotion. All of these carry with them something beautiful and human. We abstain from a thing because we have a greater love, which is why A.A. requires you to believe in God. Abstinence is a sacrifice.
>
> Moderation is the war we fight most of our lives. Balancing costs with goods requires soldierly virtues like courage, fortitude, and temperance. Everyone feels this constant struggle of indulging a little but not too much. Enjoying a drink or three, but not four. Loving your wife, but not idolizing her. Turning off a show at bedtime. Our entire existence in this age of abundance is characterized by learning moderation.
>
> Devotion is the holiest aspiration of humankind. The Greek word for humanity is “anthropos,” which means those who gaze upward.” We are defined by our pursuit of Heaven. When an Earthly good is mistaken for God, you get a Devotion to it. There is something kingly in the smoker, poetic in the drunkard, symbolic in the coffee addict, and reverent in the Libertine. This is not so with the vaper, decaf lover, N.A. beer drinker, or chronic masturbator. Devotion to the goods has a high cost, resulting in tragic beauty. Devotion to the simulacrum is free and easy.

- [Smoke Cigarettes and Die](https://jmstreetpoet.substack.com/p/smoke-cigarettes-and-die)
