> Careful Bible reading, prayer, as well as humble and receptive reading of our theological predecessors. For the rest, I don’t know that there is a “method.” Andrew Louth’s Discerning the Mystery (another great book!) has made me nervous about this term. I think different people retrieve the tradition in different ways; I doubt that there is one correct way of doing this. But one thing is clear: ressourcement cannot be done with a modern, skeptical mindset. The tradition is the playground of the Holy Spirit, and we must treat it as such.
