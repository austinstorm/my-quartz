> David Lynch had the best hair. I loved him as an artist but his hair is what I coveted. To the very end he had the most wonderful head of hair. My favorite David Lynch moment is that time during an interview he said “Eraserhead is my most spiritual movie.” To which the interviewer says, “Elaborate on that.” And Lynch simply says, “No.” It’s as funny a moment as it profound. As for me, sometimes I like to elaborate.
>
> I was sitting at a table eating dinner with some new friends and one turned to the other and said “You are amazing,” and then she proceed to give the reasons why this was so and the rest of us were stunned by the sudden torrent of blessings that were rolling off her tongue and I thought to myself “Oh this is really good.” My attention was caught. It made me think of a beautiful and gracious axiom I learned from my friend Adam Russell: “Eulogies are for the living!” We cannot wait for funerals to say all the good things we feel for our people. “When I am with you I can’t help feeling hopeful about life.” “I love who you are.” “You are a genius.” If we can say these things then why would we hold back? We are healed and we are transformed by the words that our people speak over us. It is very important that you spontaneously consecrate your family and friends with words that affirm the wonder of their created-ness, the person which God made them to be. Let us abandon our slight curses and idle pronouncements of judgement. Let us renounce words of manipulation and control and passive aggression. Let us no longer withhold the affirmations which our children, our parents, our friends, our neighbors need to hear. We might even go so crazy that we pronounce blessings on people who don’t deserve it. We might even see the image of God in our enemy and say as much. We can be fountains of life, watering the weary who may be devoid of words of life. You can bless the downtrodden. Your sweet words are strength to those who hear them. Bless your friends NOW while they are alive in the land of the living.
