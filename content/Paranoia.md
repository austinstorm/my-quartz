> There’s an old story, apocryphal, about a sanitarium up in the mountains in France. One day there was a terrible fire, and the nurses and doctors found that all of the exits were inaccessible. They panicked, yelling and screaming, sure that they were about to die. But the paranoid schizophrenics calmly led them through a complex escape route only they knew, saving the lives of everyone in the hospital. You see, schizophrenics wake up every day and ponder the ways that they might die and how they might escape death. They knew destruction was coming, and so they were ready for it. I’m always ready for my destruction too.
