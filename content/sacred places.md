"Stay away from anything   
that obscures the place it is in.   
There are no unsacred places;   
there are only sacred places   
and desecrated places."

- Wendell Berry

https://www.poetryfoundation.org/poetrymagazine/poems/41087/how-to-be-a-poet

Philosopher Giorgio Agamben quotes Trebatius Testa, a 1st-century BC Roman jurist, on irreligiosity: “In the strict sense, profane is the term for something that was once sacred or religious and is returned to the use and property of men.”
