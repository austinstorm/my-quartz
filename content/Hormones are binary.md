Metzger tells his young patients that they might not
“be binary, but hormones are binary.” He describes having
to explain to children and even adults that “you can’t get a
deeper voice without probably a bit of a beard” and “you
can’t get estrogen to feel more feminine without some
breast development.”

An endocrinologist speaking at a WPATH panel, quoted in the *WPATH Files*
