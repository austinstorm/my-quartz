English scientist James Lovelock (b. 1919) came up with the "Gaia Hypothesis", that the earth is an exceptional, self-regulating organism.

He's also pro-nuclear in a way that is (unsurprisingly) Malthusian: “We must stop fretting over the minute statistical risks of cancer from chemicals or radiation. Nearly one third of us will die of cancer anyway.”
