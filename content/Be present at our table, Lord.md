> 1. Be present at our table, Lord;
> Be here and everywhere adored;
> Thy creatures bless, and grant that we
> May feast in paradise with Thee.
>
> 2. We thank Thee, Lord, for this our food,
> For life and health and every good;
> By Thine own hand may we be fed;
> Give us each day our daily bread.
>
> 3. We thank Thee, Lord, for this our good,
> But more because of Jesus’ blood;
> Let manna to our souls be giv’n,
> The Bread of Life sent down from Heav’n.


I have a hard time finding all three verses of this, one can sing the first verse to OLD HUNDREDTH or Tallis' Canon as part of a dinner ritual. I got all three verses from the Cyber Hymnal of all places.
