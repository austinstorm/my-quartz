> Some of my fellow Catholics are worried about “bibliolatry” and I know what they mean. But at the same time, I will never stop worshipping the Bible. Some read the Bible so they can do systematic theology, I do systematic theology so I can read the Bible. I will learn every history of every culture to understand the Bible. I will learn every language and every religious tradition to more adequately comprehend the Bible. I will learn every art and science to feed its wisdom and insight into the infinite forest of the Bible. I will pray and go to mass and consume every sacrament possible to have its pages open up to me through them. I will try to enact justice and mercy on the earth so that the Holy Spirit will allow me to read the Bible. And when the eschaton comes, and I am granted the Beatific Vision, it will be inextricable from every letter and joy and tittle and material history of every redaction of my Bible. Give me that epiphanic Zoharic textual cosmos.
