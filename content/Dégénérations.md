Quebecois traditionalist song “Dégénérations”

>
> ```
>
> Your great-great-grandfather cleared a little piece of land / Your great-grandfather helped to plow the field by hand
>
> And your grandfather took it, made it grow and made it thrive / Then your father sold it off and took a steady nine-to-five
>
> As for you, young man, in the city all alone / In a one-bedroom loft winter chills you to the bone
>
> When you dream at night you feel the shovel in your hand / As you walk the fields of that little piece of land
> ```
>
