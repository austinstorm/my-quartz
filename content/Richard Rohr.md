> The things I teach come from a combination of inner and outer authority, drawn from personal experience and a long lineage of the “perennial tradition” as Aldous Huxley, Huston Smith, Ken Wilber, and many others have called it.


https://cac.org/daily-meditations/the-perennial-tradition-explore-divine-reality-2015-12-20/

Rohr gets dumber the more clearly he talks about his own project. The Transcendentalists and the Universalists, followed-on by the Perennialists Rohr lists, did violence to the hermetic tradition. They are 'weak tea', but derive what rhetorical power they have by invoking perennial concepts. One is better off with the Traditionalists.
