In 1922, Ecumenical Patriarch Meletius IV of Constantinople issued an encyclical recognizing Anglican Holy Orders as valid, a view later endorsed by several other Orthodox Churches (Jerusalem, Cyprus, Alexandria, Romania)

Didn't hold for very long, but interesting ecumenical historical trivia
