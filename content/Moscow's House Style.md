Doug Wilson's words have been very *generative*, which is to say a lot of stuff has come to pass largely because of his writing output.

He's read a lot of Chesterton and Wodehouse and is very aphoristic, but ultimately has his own thing going which reminds me of a mix of Garrison Keillor and Red Green.

All that to say, when people come to Moscow and want to be part of the apparatus, they often imitate Doug's writing style. This has three qualities: playing cultural connect-the-dots, being aphoristic, a certain pervasive 'jauntiness'.

No one has exemplified this more than Toby Sumpter. He auditioned to be on the Moscow Brain Trust harder than anyone I've known, and it seems to have paid off. Examples abound, but I'm generally interested in documenting it further afield.

> "We’ve been paying premiums for decades for ripped jeans and torn looks. It cannot come as a great shock when we begin paying doctors to try to rip our genes with hormone therapies and genital mutilation. Ripped & torn, nipped & tucked, we hate the image of God."
