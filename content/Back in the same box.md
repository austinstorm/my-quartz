"Once the game is over, the king and the pawn go back into the same box." - *Italian proverb*

'At our meetings, our powerlessness puts us all in the same box. In the sight of God we are all equal - and status games which have seemed so important are ultimately silly.'

The ludic nature of AA contrasted with the ludic nature of the liturgy. In AA one's identity is subsumed by one's status as an addict. "My name is, and I'm an alcoholic." In the Eucharist one doesn't entirely lose one's identity, because of the importance of being able to show 'greater honor' (or to bestow modesty) to people of lower status.

John talked about Huizinga's classic *Homo Ludens* in his commencement address at Theopolis.
