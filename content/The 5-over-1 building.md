> In America, there’s a single shape that proliferates like a yeast infection across the country: the “5-over-1” building, consisting of five wood-framed stories of poky apartments over a concrete podium, all wrapped up in plastic cladding. In every city these gray “luxury” blocks are as inescapable and as identical as the Soviet Union’s khrushchevkas. The universality of this form doesn’t even point to any universal approach to how life ought to be lived: it’s a pure figment of the U.S.’s fire safety codes. This shape gives you the highest density of residential units for the lowest investment while still being technically legal. That’s it.


- Sam Kriss, https://damagemag.com/2023/12/12/a-great-satan-in-this-grave/
