**The Seven Rules of Wisdom**

> Do not all that you can, for that is pride.
> Ask not all that you know not, for that is curiosity.
> Believe not all that you hear, for that is credulity.
> Give not all that you have, for that is prodigality.
> Tell not all that you know, for that is folly.
> Judge not all that you see, for that is impudence.
> Desire not all that you like, for that is stupidity.
