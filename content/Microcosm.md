Claude Lévi-Strauss, visiting the Bororo, noticed that “their wise men have worked out an impressive cosmology and embodied it in the plan of the villages and the layout of the dwellings.”

Source: https://damagemag.com/2023/12/12/a-great-satan-in-this-grave/

[[C.S. Lewis on Richard Hooker]] said that "Every system offers us a model of the universe."

I love the idea that each institution, and each city / settlement is the universe in microcosm. A cosmology.
