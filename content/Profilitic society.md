> In a sincerity-based society, such as the traditional family or traditional churches, we prove that we’re good to those near to us by enthusiastically fulfilling the roles assigned to us, and casting dispersion on those who don’t. In an authenticity-based society, we receive recognition by proving how original and creative we are. But in a profilitic society, we are seeking to prove our worth to anonymous crowds, by the ever shifting standards that anonymous crowd may hold. Day after day, we constantly offer up self-portraits and autobiographies in the hope of something catching and our social worth being proven.
