"We have no doctrine of our own—we only possess the Catholic doctrine of the Catholic Church enshrined in the Catholic creeds, and those creeds we hold without addition or diminution. We stand firm on that rock."

- Most Rev. Geoffrey Fisher

Worth noting that he was more Evangelical than Anglo-Catholic, in the British 20th-century sense

“If we are to be guided by Scripture, antiquity, and the spirit of the Reformers we shall take as our title that of the American Catholic Church, or the Catholic Church in the United States.”

- Right Rev. Charles C. Grafton

Grafton you can definitely say was Anglo-Catholic in the post-Oxford movement sense
