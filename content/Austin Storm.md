My name is Austin Storm. Here are a few things you might want to know, or [[chuck|maybe you just forgot]].

I like [[entrepreneurship|starting businesses]] more than running them. I try to temper my restlessness by forcing myself to follow through.

I am [[My personality type|optimistic]], but prone to bouts of [[Depression|depression]].

As a child I decided that success meant orange juice with every breakfast and haircuts every other week. By that measure, I have not yet arrived.

I dislike [[The telephone was an aberration|the telephone]].

I prefer liquor to beer, and gin is my favorite liquor.

I grew up in [[Annapolis, Maryland]] and now live in Colfax, Washington.

I think I would be very happy if I was able to [[projects|start a new business]] every few years for the rest of my life.
