> As seriously as the ordained clergy need to take their ordination, so you
> ought to as church singers. Canonically, they are an order of the Church,
> to begin with. I’m not proposing that we fulfill the letter of the law by
> having you all ordained, but I think we ought to at least incarnate the
> spirit of the law, which implies a great responsibility, a great sense of
> duty and a privilege that is given to him or her as a church singer. This,
> then, should create in all of us, whether or not we are ordained clergy, a
> real sense of humility. We should give thanks that God has been pleased
> to call us who were created from the dust of this earth to participate in
> the heavenly liturgy and to offer up praises with His angels to join the
> perpetual hymn of “Holy, holy, holy.”
