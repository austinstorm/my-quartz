> When we battle a bad habit…simply not doing it is seldom effective. Something good must be done in its place. Life is best lived in the active voice. Not surprisingly, there is an active voice in the practice of humility. The giving of thanks is humility in the active voice.


- Fr. Stephen Freeman, from *Face to Face: Knowing God beyond Our Shame*
