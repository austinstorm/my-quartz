> ...the “typical” kind of psychic problem in our day is not hysteria, as it was in Freud’s time, but the schizoid type-that is to say, the problem of persons who are detached, unrelated, lacking in affect, tending towards depersonalization, and covering up their problems by means of intellectualizations and technical formulations.... There is also plenty of evidence that the sense of isolation, the alienation of one’s self from the world is suffered not only by people in pathological conditions, but by countless “normal” persons as well in our day.


- Rollo May
