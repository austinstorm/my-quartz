> A woman can feel dramatically more comfortable after surgically altering her female body without the improvement proving that she was, in some meaningful sense, male all along. People obtain real psychological relief from all sorts of interventions because the intervention changes their relationship to themselves. Sometimes the explanation they attach to that relief is correct. Sometimes it isn’t.

And *Pageboy* repeatedly treats those two questions as though they were one.

Page suffered terribly. She changed her body and the story through which she understood herself. She subsequently felt better. That is evidence that the intervention relieved some of her suffering.

It is not evidence of metaphysical maleness.

- Holly Mathnerd [The Past Is Immutable](https://hollymathnerd.substack.com/p/the-past-is-immutable)
