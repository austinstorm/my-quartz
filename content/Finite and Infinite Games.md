from Plato’s *Laws*:

> “I say that a man must be serious with the serious. God alone is worthy of supreme seriousness, but man is made God’s plaything and that is the best part of him. Therefore every man and woman should live life accordingly, and play the noblest games and be of another mind from what they are at present.”
