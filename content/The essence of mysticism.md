> The essence of mysticism is a deep inner sense of God’s transforming presence in your life that increases your love of God and your love of neighbor. God’s gratuitous gift, which Catholics used to call sanctifying grace, is the essence. The special graces, such as visions, ecstasies, and the like, are granted only to a few—not for their own sake, but for the sake of manifesting the powers of grace to the whole community of believers. So mysticism is the fructifying of the baptismal grace that every Christian receives.


- Bernard McGinn
