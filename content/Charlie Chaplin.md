Specifically, Walter Benjamin on Charlie Chaplin:

> Chaplin is a consistent mismatch with any circumstance in which he happens to find himself; he is out of line with every social world. But, therefore, he embodies a nobility of spirit: ‘the world fits him like a glove.’ Wherever he is, it is as though his surroundings are tailored to his character. Chaplin shows a life where there is a peculiar fit to the world, come what may, even in always failing. That this makes us double up in laughter, rather than bemoan his terrible fate, is what justifies Benjamin in calling Chaplin ‘the only angel of peace who is suited to this world.’
