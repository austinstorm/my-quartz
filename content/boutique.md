Boutique is one of those words like '[[hipster]]' or '[[woke]]' that have, y'know, actual denotative content but function as pejoratives or status markers in a way that obscures their useful meaning.

https://onethingnewsletter.substack.com/p/is-boutique-a-bad-word

There are stores that are boutiques. But if you start a store and call it a 'boutique' this is probably an aspirational self-designation that will hurt you more than help you. It unintentionally communicates the wrong thing. If you were actually a boutique you would be sophisticated enough to know not to call yourself a boutique.
