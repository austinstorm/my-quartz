I forgot that American Anglo-Catholics (in particular those from Nashotah-house, who are trained to be allergic to it) have a strong bias against the 1662, and that it's a marker of being Reformed/Calvinist in the US.

This, for instance, seems like a funny take: https://anglicancatholicliturgyandtheology.wordpress.com/2021/05/22/problems-with-1662/

Not the author's desire to avoid novelty by choosing something not explicitly authorized, but that one get what one wants through an assemblage of the existing authorized prayer books.

The author, Mark David Haveland, is similarly catty about the BCP2019. But I find that I more or less agree with him!
