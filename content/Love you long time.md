“If you’ve loved anybody that long, first as an infant, then as a child, then as a man . . . [y]ou gain a strange perspective on time and human pain and effort. Other people cannot see what I see whenever I look into your father’s face, for behind your father’s face as it is today are all those other faces which were his.”

- James Baldwin, on his brother
