nudi nudum Christum sequi — “nakedly following the naked Christ”

Precursors to the Latin phrase *nudi nudum Christum sequi* date back to St. Jerome in the 4th century. For instance, in Jerome’s Letter 125, he writes to Rusticus, “Naked, follow the naked cross of Christ.” The sense is clearly a response to Jesus’ call to the rich young ruler: “If you want to be perfect, go, sell what you have and give to the poor, and you will have treasure in heaven; and come, follow Me” (Mt 19:21). Jesus calls his students to strip off all that holds us back from total abandon to the fully abandoned Jesus.
