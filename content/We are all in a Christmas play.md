> We are, every one of us, in a Christmas play. Be a shepherd, be a wise man, be an angel. Don't be King Herod. Your heart is a cave, waiting for the Fullness to be born and laid softly within. If you are a ox or even an ass, you are intived to adore the Child who comes to heal us and restore us to the innocence of a child (and more). Adeste Fidelis!


- Fr. Stephen Freeman
