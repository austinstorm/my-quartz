> “What we need is the rekindling of desire - the sheer passionate longing to see a social order at which the Holy Lamb of God might look without heartbreak. Arrows of desire; the courage and endurance of mental fight; the struggle to keep this imagination alive and burning - this is what we pray for. The poem [Jerusalem] looks back to an imaginary past and forward to an imagined future, but at its heart is the question: 'do you truly want to live in Jerusalem? Because if you do, you need to remember that it is always already here and now; because even where justice and love seem to be defeated, the Holy Lamb of God is present.’”


— Rowan Williams
